# 擲硬幣圖片素材

使用內建 `image_gen` 生成，正反面各生成一張透明背景 PNG，原始檔已複製至專案根目錄。

- 正面：`coin-10-heads.png`
- 反面：`coin-10-tails.png`
- 使用頁面：`CoinFlip.html`
- 外觀參考：[中央銀行：中華民國建國一百年流通拾圓硬幣](https://www.cbc.gov.tw/public/data/issue/money/A2/100th10.htm)。圖片為生成的遊戲素材。

## 正面生成提示詞

Use case: product-mockup. Asset: transparent PNG obverse sprite for an animated coin flip web game. Generate exactly ONE modern Taiwan New Taiwan Dollar 10 dollar coin (2011 Sun Yat-sen portrait version), front face, perfectly circular and centered, orthographic straight-on view, no perspective tilt. Silver nickel metal, realistic embossed relief bust of Sun Yat-sen facing left, rim engraved Traditional Chinese '中華民國100年' across the top. Entire coin occupies 90 percent of square canvas with equal transparent margins. Crisp rim and subtle metallic surface patina, soft studio light upper left, no cast shadow outside coin. No other coins, no hands, no props, no captions or watermark. Genuine transparent background. Photorealistic but readable as a game sprite.

## 反面生成提示詞

Use case: product-mockup. Asset: transparent PNG reverse sprite for an animated coin flip web game. Generate exactly ONE modern Taiwan New Taiwan Dollar 10 dollar coin reverse (2011 modern Sun Yat-sen series). Perfectly circular centered orthographic straight-on view, no perspective tilt. Silver nickel metal realistic embossed relief, large prominent embossed Arabic '10' and Chinese '圓' lower center; small Taiwan island silhouette inside the zero; ornamental ruyi shaped frame across upper face with subtle '國泰民安' detail; small raised tactile dot near bottom rim. Match same diameter, centered composition and lighting as a front sprite. Entire coin occupies 90 percent of square canvas with equal transparent margins. Crisp rim, subtle patina, soft studio light upper left, no cast shadow outside coin. No portrait on reverse. No other coins, no hands, no props, no captions or watermark. Genuine transparent background. Photorealistic but readable as a game sprite.
